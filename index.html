Ask Lovora on Instagram ↗
What do I need to send you?
Start with the person’s name, occasion, preferred colours or theme, your message, and the photos you want to use. You can also share reference links or ideas, even if they’re still a little messy.
How much does a website cost?
Every request can be a little different, so send us your idea on Instagram. We’ll confirm the features and quote before starting. A custom domain or other third-party costs may change the total.
Can I request my own domain name?
Yes, ask about a custom domain when you message us. Availability, registration, renewal costs and setup options need to be confirmed for the domain you want.
Will it work on phones and computers?
The pages are designed responsively for modern mobile and desktop browsers. If you have a special device or feature requirement, mention it in your brief.
Can I use my own images and words?
Absolutely — your photos and message are what make the page personal. Please send media you own or have permission to use, and let us know which photos or words should be kept private.
How do I place an order?
Open the lovora.wish Instagram profile and send a DM with your occasion, preferred theme, deadline and the main features you have in mind. We’ll take it from there.
